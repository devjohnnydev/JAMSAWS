Analyze the results In regression problems like the abalone age prediction, two commonly used evaluation metrics are Mean Squared Error (MSE) and Standard Deviation (STD): Mean Squared Error (MSE): - Measures the average squared difference between predicted and actual values - Lower MSE values indicate better model performance - Heavily penalizes large errors due to squaring - Always non-negative, with 0 being perfect prediction Standard Deviation (STD): - Measures the amount of variation or dispersion in predictions - Calculated as the square root of the variance - Indicates how spread out the predictions are from their mean - Lower STD suggests more consistent predictions - Useful for understanding prediction reliability and model stability For the abalone age prediction problem, these metrics help us understand both the accuracy (MSE) and consistency (STD) of our model's predictions.
